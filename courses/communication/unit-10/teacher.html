EFC Unit 10: Let's Find a New Apartment — Teacher Guide

Timed 120-minute lesson plan, Kahoot question bank, gesture notes, and common problems. This site has no login, so treat this page's URL as unlisted, not truly private. Do not share the link with students.

Lesson at a Glance

Goal: students compare three apartments, listen for details and opinions, take pros/cons notes, and decide which apartment Karen should choose. Every step lets students point, choose, match, or discuss with a partner before they must speak alone.

Student page: index.html (11 screens). Audio: the same 5 licensed Q: Skills for Success recordings as before. Nothing about the audio was changed or invented.

How to read the table below. On the site means students do it on their own device or on the projector. Live in class means you run it with no screen needed. Outside the site means you prepare it yourself (Kahoot). Timings are approximate. Move on when most pairs are ready.

Vocabulary Gestures (Step 3)

Do the gesture first, say the word, then students copy. This lets students show they understand without speaking.

Accessibility Notes

  • One instruction at a time. Say it, show it, wait. Repeat the same words each time ("Point. Choose. Talk to your partner.").
  • Think time. Give 15 to 20 seconds of silent think time, then partner talk, then whole class. Never cold call.
  • Speaking is never the only option. Every speaking step has a point, click, or show-the-answer alternative.
  • Sentence frames are on screen in Steps 4, 8, and 10. Put them on the board as well.
  • Audio: play each recording at least twice when students struggle. Stop and replay a short part if a pair is lost.
  • The student pages carry teacher notes in Sections 5 and 8 (teal-edged box). Cover them if you project the page and prefer not to show them.

Common Problems

Students go silent in the warm-up.
Fix: use point-or-click only for the first two pairs. Add the words after students are comfortable.
The matching task (Section 4) feels too hard.
Fix: replay the audio. Ask partners to compare their notes first. Give the Section 3 table back as a hint for distance and rent.
Everyone picks the same apartment in the group decision.
Fix: that is fine. Ask groups to give a different reason from the next group.
Class is running long.
Fix: see "If you run behind" under the schedule.
Students are on mobile phones and the table is hard to read.
Fix: project the apartment table in Section 3 and let pairs read from the board.
