EFC Unit 10: What Makes a Good Home? Teacher Guide

Timed 120-minute plan, optional Kahoot bank, gesture notes, and common problems for the A1 version of this unit. This site has no login, so treat this page's URL as unlisted, not truly private. Do not share the link with students.

Lesson at a Glance

Goal: A1 students use simple English to name rooms, describe a home, say what they like and want, give one reason with because, choose between homes, and present their own "My Perfect Home".

Path: LOOK, LEARN, CHOOSE, PRACTICE, BUILD, DRAW, SPEAK, PRESENT. Every screen has a short instruction (Look. Click. Listen.) and a picture. Students can complete every activity by clicking. Nothing needs typing.

Student page: index.html (10 sections). Core words (about 30, all repeated): bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, living room, dining room, garden, balcony, big, small, clean, dirty, quiet, noisy, safe, beautiful, comfortable, cheap, expensive, modern, old, and the frames I like / I want / I don't want ... because ...

Audio: the Listen buttons use the browser's built-in English voice (American). The long housing-policy recordings from the old version are no longer in the lesson. The audio files are still in assets/audio/comm-unit10 if you want them for another class.

Support for A1 Students

  • One instruction at a time. Say it, show it, wait. Repeat the same words each time: "Look. Click. Listen. Say it."
  • Say it. Sections 1, 4, 5, 6, and 9 end with a sentence students read aloud. Count these as speaking practice: pairs say each sentence two times.
  • Repetition is on purpose. The same words come back in every section. Do not add new words.
  • There is no wrong answer in Sections 1, 6, 7, and 9 (opinions and choices). Praise a complete sentence.
  • Wrong answers in the games show "Try again." and students can keep clicking. The score shows first-try answers only.
  • Spelling: American English (favorite, color).

Final Project Link

"My Perfect Home" is the individual practice for the Unit 11 project, Design a Home and Give a Presentation. In Unit 11, students work in groups of 3 to 4. Students bring their Unit 10 drawing and script and use them to talk about the group home. Unit 11 itself was not changed.

Presentation model (Section 9): Hello. This is my perfect home. It has three bedrooms. It has two bathrooms. It has a big kitchen. It has a garden. My favorite room is my bedroom. I like it because it is comfortable. Thank you.

Common Problems

Students do not say anything.
Fix: use click answers first, then repeat the sentence together as a class, then in pairs.
Listen button is silent.
Fix: the voice comes from the browser. Check the device volume. Chrome and Safari work best.
Student cannot print the worksheet.
Fix: use blank paper. The 5 sentences are on the screen in Section 8.
Student skipped Build Your Home.
Fix: Section 9 shows a button to go back to Section 7 and then builds the script.
