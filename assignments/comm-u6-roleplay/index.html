Real-Life English: Ordering Food at a Restaurant

Go to a real restaurant with your group and use English to order real food.

Due August 23, 2026

How to Submit

Send your video, or a link to your video, in our OpenChat group: EFC_Group 28.

Deadline of your videos: August 23, 2026 11:59 PM.

A submit button will appear on this page once student accounts are ready. For now, please use OpenChat.

What Is This Task?

This is your chance to use English outside the classroom. Go to a real restaurant, café, or food court with your group and record yourselves ordering real food in English. You practiced this conversation in the Unit 6 Speaking Task. Now use it for real.

Your Group

Work in a group of 3 or 4 students. Groups of 3 are preferred, but 4 is also fine.

  • Every student must speak English in the video.
  • No student should only hold the camera and stay silent.
  • If your group has 4 students, all 4 should speak in the conversation.

SPEAK ENGLISH

The goal of this assignment is to give you a real chance to practice speaking English. Every video you submit will be accepted and graded. Speak slowly, use the expressions from Unit 6, and try your best to communicate your order in English.

All group members must speak English in the video.

The Real Restaurant Video

Visit a real restaurant, café, food court, or similar place. Order real food that you actually want to eat. This is not a role play, you should visit a real restaurant or food stall.

The restaurant staff do not need to speak English. If they answer in Thai, that is okay. What matters is your English, not theirs.

What You Should Say

You do not need a long conversation. Simple, real English is enough.

Hello.
I'd like fried rice, please.
Can I have iced tea, please?
I'd like chicken noodles, please.
That's all, thank you.

Use the Unit 6 model conversation to prepare, and choose real food you actually want to order.

Video Requirements

Recommended length: about 1 to 2 minutes. Keep it short.

  1. Show the students at the restaurant.
  2. Show each student speaking English.
  3. Show students choosing or ordering food.
  4. Show students ordering a drink or another item.
  5. End politely, for example with "Thank you."

Tips for Your Video

  • Practice your sentences before you go.
  • Use the expressions from Unit 6.
  • Speak slowly and clearly.
  • Keep your English simple.
  • Don't worry about small mistakes.
  • Make sure everyone speaks.
  • Make sure the audio is clear.
  • Be polite to the restaurant staff.
  • Focus on using English, not making a perfect video.

Grading Criteria

You are graded mainly on speaking, not on perfect grammar. Small grammar mistakes are fine if your meaning is clear.

English Speaking (50%)
Speaks English during the real restaurant interaction.
Communication (20%)
Communicates the food order clearly enough to be understood.
Pronunciation and Clarity (15%)
Speaks clearly and understandably.
Participation (15%)
All group members participate and speak English.

The most important thing is that you speak English and try to communicate your order. Every submitted video is accepted and graded.

Before You Submit

  • You went to a real restaurant, café, or food court.
  • Every group member spoke English in the video.
  • You ordered real food using simple English.
  • Your video is about 1 to 2 minutes long.
  • Your video and audio are clear.
