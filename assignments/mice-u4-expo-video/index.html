MICE Expo Simulation Video

Meet a stranger at a professional expo, make a great first impression, and exchange business cards, the way real MICE professionals do it.

Due August 21, 2026

How to Submit

Send your video, or a link to your video, in our OpenChat group: MICE_WTM.

A submit button will appear on this page once student accounts are ready. For now, please use OpenChat.

What Is This Task?

With a partner, record a role-play as if you are meeting for the first time at a MICE expo. Use the greeting, small talk, and card-exchange language from Unit 4 to make a strong, professional first impression.

How the Conversation Goes

Record ONE video. Follow this order, but keep it sounding natural, not like a script.

  1. Greet your partner as if meeting for the first time at the expo.
  2. Introduce yourself: your name, and a made-up company or role.
  3. Make small talk. Use at least 2 of the 8 pro networking tips naturally.
  4. Use "...I like the ___ because..." at least once (the Because Bridge).
  5. Ask one icebreaker question and keep the conversation going.
  6. End by professionally offering your business card.

Use Your Unit 4 Skills

This is exactly what Unit 4 practiced. Bring it into the video.

  • Look at your partner. Not the camera, not the floor.
  • Smile and offer a confident handshake.
  • Use each other's name after you're introduced.
  • Use the Because Bridge naturally, not forced.
  • Ask a real icebreaker question and actually listen to the answer.
  • Offer your business card professionally, with both hands or a clear gesture.

Video Requirements

  • Work with a partner.
  • Record ONE video of the full conversation.
  • Show both students in the same video the whole time.
  • Speak naturally. Do not just read from a script.
  • Keep your video about 2 to 4 minutes long.
  • Professional clothing is optional for your video presentation.

Speaking Rubric

You are graded on how naturally and professionally you communicate.

Fluency
Speak smoothly. Try not to stop too much.
Pronunciation
Speak clearly so your partner and teacher can understand you.
Vocabulary
Use greeting, small talk, and networking phrases from the unit.
Interaction
Listen and respond to your partner. Do not just say lines.
Professionalism
Be polite, confident, and friendly the whole time.

Before You Submit

  • You greeted your partner and introduced yourself.
  • You used at least 2 of the 8 pro networking tips.
  • You used the Because Bridge at least once.
  • You asked an icebreaker question and kept the conversation going.
  • You ended by professionally offering your business card.
  • Your video and audio are clear.
