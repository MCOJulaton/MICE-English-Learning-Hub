"A Day in My Life" Vlog

Film yourself narrating your daily routine in English, just like a real "day in my life" video.

Due August 14, 2026

How to Submit

Send your video, or a link to your video, in our OpenChat group: EFC_Group 28.

A submit button will appear on this page once student accounts are ready. For now, please use OpenChat.

What Is This Task?

Film a 1–2 minute solo vlog narrating your typical day in English, from waking up to going to bed. Talk directly to the camera like you're vlogging for an audience, not reading a report.

What To Include

  1. Open with a hook, a friendly line that pulls viewers in.
  2. Narrate your morning routine using sequencing words (first, then, after that).
  3. Narrate at least 3 different times of day (e.g. morning, afternoon, evening).
  4. Use a transition phrase to move between parts of your day.
  5. Close with an outro, a short, friendly sign-off.

Use Your Unit 5 Skills

This is exactly what Unit 5 practiced. Bring it into the video.

  • Use at least 4 routine phrases from the unit (wake up, get ready, attend class, review notes...).
  • Use at least 2 vlog phrases: a hook, a transition, or an outro.
  • Mention at least 3 different times of day.
  • Look at the camera and speak naturally, like you're talking to a friend.

Video Requirements

  • Solo video, just you talking to the camera.
  • Length: 1–2 minutes.
  • Speak naturally. Do not just read from a script.
  • Any location is fine, home, school, wherever your day happens.

Speaking Rubric

You are graded on how naturally and clearly you communicate.

Fluency
Speak smoothly. Try not to stop too much.
Pronunciation
Speak clearly enough that a viewer can follow along.
Vocabulary
Use routine phrases, sequencing words, and vlog phrases.
Structure
Clear hook, body, and outro, in that order.
Delivery
Energetic and friendly, like a real vlog.

Before You Submit

  • Your video is 1 to 2 minutes long.
  • You used at least 4 routine phrases.
  • You used at least 2 vlog phrases (hook, transition, or outro).
  • You mentioned at least 3 different times of day.
  • You spoke naturally. You did not just read from a script.
  • Your video and audio are clear.
