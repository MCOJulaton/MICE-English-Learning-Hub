Final Challenge: You Are the MICE Staff

Act as MICE staff. Welcome a delegate, talk with them, and give directions around the event.

Due August 20, 2026 · 11:59 PM

How to Submit

Send your video, or a link to your video, in our OpenChat group: MICE_WTM.

A submit button will appear on this page once student accounts are ready. For now, please use OpenChat.

What Is This Task?

You and your partner will act out a MICE event. One of you is the staff, and one of you is the delegate. The staff member welcomes the delegate, talks with them a little, and gives them directions. Focus on making yourself understood, listening to your partner, and keeping the conversation going.

The Two Rounds

Record ONE video with both rounds. Both students play both roles.

How the Conversation Goes

  1. Say a friendly, professional greeting.
  2. Give a short introduction. Say your name and your role.
  3. Have a short, natural chat before you start (small talk).
  4. The delegate asks for directions.
  5. The MICE staff gives clear directions.
  6. The MICE staff asks if the delegate needs more help.
  7. End the conversation politely.
Student A: MICE Staff → Student B: Delegate

Student B asks for directions to:

1. Ballroom A (Main Plenary Hall)
2. Registration Desk
3. Nearest Restrooms
4. Catering / Lunch Area

This is an example to help you. Use your own name. Do not just read it word for word.

(smiles, offers a handshake)

B · Delegate
(shakes hand)

A · Staff

A · Staff
Of course! What are you looking for first?
B · Delegate
Could you tell me where Ballroom A is? The main plenary hall?
A · Staff

B · Delegate

A · Staff

B · Delegate
And the nearest restrooms?
A · Staff
They're just past the reception desk, on your left.
B · Delegate
Perfect. One more thing. Where's the catering or lunch area?
A · Staff
Take the elevator to the second floor and follow the signs for Delegate Dining.
B · Delegate
Thank you so much. This is really helpful!
A · Staff

B · Delegate

A · Staff

B · Delegate
Thank you, you too!

Use Your Unit 4 Skills

This is where your Unit 4 greeting skills come back.

  • Look at your partner. Not the camera, not the floor.
  • Smile when you greet each other.
  • Give a firm handshake at the start of each round.
  • Use each other's name.
  • Have a short, natural chat before you start.
  • Stand tall and look confident.

Video Requirements

  • Work with a partner.
  • Record ONE video with both rounds.
  • Both students speak and both students play both roles.
  • Show both students in the same video the whole time. Do not record separately.
  • Act like you are really at a MICE event.
  • Speak naturally. Do not just read from a script.
  • Keep your video about 3 to 5 minutes long.
  • Professional clothing is optional for your video presentation.

Speaking Rubric

You are graded on how naturally and professionally you communicate. Giving the exact right directions is not the main goal.

Fluency
Speak smoothly. Try not to stop too much.
Pronunciation
Speak clearly so your partner and teacher can understand you.
Vocabulary
Use direction words and polite greeting phrases.
Interaction
Listen and respond to your partner. Do not just say lines.
Professionalism
Be polite and friendly the whole time.

Before You Submit

  • Both rounds are in the same video, in the right order.
  • Both students played both roles: MICE Staff and Delegate.
  • All 4 directions were asked and given in each round.
  • Your video is about 3 to 5 minutes long.
  • You spoke naturally. You did not just read from a script.
  • Your video and audio are clear.
