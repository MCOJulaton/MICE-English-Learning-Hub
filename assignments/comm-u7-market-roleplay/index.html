Food Market Role-Play

Buy food at a market in English. One of you sells, one of you buys. Then switch.

Due September 4, 2026

How to Submit

Send your video, or a link to your video, in our OpenChat group: EFC_Group 28.

A submit button will appear on this page once student accounts are ready. For now, please use OpenChat.

What Is This Task?

With a partner, act out buying food at a market. One of you is the Customer, one of you is the Seller. Then you switch roles and do it again. This is the final task for Unit 7, and it uses everything you practiced: market words, "I want to," and asking about price.

Your Mission

  1. Buy 3 different foods.
  2. Stay under 200 baht.
  3. Use English with your partner.

The Roles

Customer → Seller

Customer says:

Hello.
I'd like ___, please.
How much is it?
Anything else? No, thank you.
Thank you.

Seller says:

Hello. Can I help you?
How many?
Anything else?
It's ___ baht.
Thank you. Have a nice day.

This is a model to help you. Use your own words too. You don't need to memorize one fixed script.

Before You Perform

  • Choose your foods.
  • Check your budget (3 items, under 200 baht).
  • Prepare your conversation with your partner.
  • Practice it once before recording.

Video Requirements

  • Work with a partner.
  • Record ONE video, two rounds. Switch roles between rounds.
  • Show both students in the same video the whole time.
  • Speak naturally. Do not just read from a script.
  • Keep your video about 2 to 4 minutes long.

Speaking Rubric

You are graded on how naturally and clearly you communicate, not on getting the "perfect" price.

Fluency
Speak smoothly. Try not to stop too much.
Pronunciation
Speak clearly so your partner and teacher can understand you.
Vocabulary
Use market words and the customer/seller phrases.
Interaction
Listen and respond to your partner. Do not just say lines.
Mission
3 different foods, under 200 baht.

Before You Submit

  • Both students played both roles: Customer and Seller.
  • You bought 3 different foods.
  • You stayed under 200 baht.
  • You spoke naturally. You did not just read from a script.
  • Your video and audio are clear.
