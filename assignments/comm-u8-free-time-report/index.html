Free-Time Report Video

Ask 4 classmates about their free time. Then tell us what you found.

What Is This Task?

You practiced the Free-Time Activity Hunt in class. Now make it a video. Ask 4 classmates what they do in their free time and why they like it. Then report what you found. You work alone, but you talk about your classmates.

Your Mission

  1. Ask 4 classmates: "What do you do in your free time?" and "Why do you like it?"
  2. Write keywords only on your worksheet.
  3. Record a 1 to 2 minute video: report on all 4 classmates.
  4. End with your own preference: indoor or outdoor, and why.

What You Say

A model to help you. Use your own words too.

I talked to four classmates.
May likes watching movies because it is relaxing.
Bank likes playing games because it is fun.
Fon likes listening to music because it is interesting.
I prefer outdoor activities because I like sports.

Remember: he or she + -s (likes, plays, goes). Use do, play and go with the right activity: do yoga, play games, go shopping.

Video Requirements

  • Work alone. Your face and voice must be in the video.
  • Keep your video 1 to 2 minutes long.
  • Report on 4 classmates. Give a reason for each one.
  • Speak from your keywords. Do not read full sentences.
  • Say the names of your classmates, or use "my classmate."

Speaking Rubric

You are graded on how clearly you communicate, not on perfect grammar.

Interview
You asked 4 classmates and gave a reason for each.
do / play / go
Right in at least 3 of 4 sentences.
Reasons and -s
"Bank likes playing games because it is fun."
Preference
"I prefer ___ activities because ___."
Not read
You speak from keywords, with your eyes up.

Before You Submit

  • You asked 4 classmates.
  • You gave a reason for each one.
  • You said your own preference.
  • You did not just read from a script.
  • Your video and audio are clear.
