Agree or Disagree Video

Talk about a trend with your seatmate. One agrees, one disagrees.

What Is This Task?

You practiced agree and disagree chats in class. Now record one. With your seatmate, choose a topic. One of you agrees and one of you disagrees. Each of you gives one simple reason.

How to Do It

  1. Sit with your seatmate and choose one topic below.
  2. Plan with keywords. One of you agrees, the other disagrees.
  3. Use pronouns correctly: I, he, she, we, they, and me, him, her, us, them.
  4. Use an agree or disagree phrase. Give one simple reason with "because".
  5. Practice two times. Then record. 30 to 60 seconds is enough.

Choose a Topic

  • Everyone should learn how to use AI.
  • Social media is good for teenagers.
  • Online classes are better than classroom classes.
  • Students should use their phones in class.
  • K-pop is the best music right now.
  • Fast food is bad for your health.
  • Working from home is better than going to the office.
  • Short videos, like TikTok, are better than long videos.

Video Requirements

  • Work with your seatmate. Both of you speak.
  • Keep your video 30 to 60 seconds long.
  • Look at each other. Do not read a long script.
  • Your video and audio are clear.

Speaking Rubric

Each row is worth up to 20 points: 20 completely successful, 15 mostly, 10 partly, 0 not successful.

Clear opinion
It is clear who agrees and who disagrees.
Pronouns
I / me, he / him, she / her, we / us, they / them, used correctly.
Agree or disagree phrase
"Me too," "I don't either," "I'm not sure about that," and so on.
One reason
A short, understandable reason with "because".
Natural sound
Him, her and them said short and natural.

Before You Submit

  • Each of you gave a clear opinion.
  • You used pronouns correctly.
  • You used an agree or disagree phrase and one reason.
  • You did not just read from a script.
  • Your video is 30 to 60 seconds.
