What this is

The English Learning Hub is a collection of self-paced, interactive English lessons. Each unit combines short readings, vocabulary practice, listening (with built-in text-to-speech), speaking prompts, and short activities that check your understanding as you go. Everything runs right in the browser, so there's nothing to install.

Courses

English for MICE: English for Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibitions. Covers professional greetings and networking, and giving directions at events.

English for Communication: everyday spoken English. Covers daily routines and cultural studies around food and eating.

English for Wellness Tourism: English for spa, resort, and wellness tourism professionals. Its units open one at a time, right before each class session.

Business Communication: professional English for meetings, email, presentations, and the workplace. Its units are still being planned.

How progress is tracked

When you check in to a unit, that check-in, your activity completions, and your scores are recorded so your teacher can see participation and progress. Simply opening a page is never counted as attendance. Only an explicit check-in counts.

What's next

Student accounts, saved progress across sessions, and assignment submission are planned for a future update. The Assignments page already shows real assignments from your units so you know what's coming due, even before submission goes live.
